Article I: Purpose

The purpose of these rules is to ensure fair competition, athlete safety, and the continued integrity of the sport of underwater basket weaving. All participants, coaches, officials, and volunteers are expected to uphold the principles of sportsmanship, respect, and excellence.

Article II: Team Composition

Section 2.1 – Roster Requirements

  • Teams shall consist of two (2) to four (4) active competitors.
  • Each team may designate up to two (2) Team Captains.
  • Teams may register up to two (2) substitute competitors.

Section 2.2 – Eligibility

  • Competitors must be at least 16 years of age.
  • All participants must complete a liability waiver prior to competition.
  • Competitors must demonstrate basic swimming proficiency.

Article III: Equipment Standards

Section 3.1 – Basket Materials

Approved materials include:

  • Reed
  • Rattan
  • Willow
  • Cane
  • Other materials approved by the Competition Committee

Section 3.2 – Diving Equipment

Competitors may use:

  • Masks
  • Snorkels
  • Fins
  • Exposure suits

The use of underwater propulsion devices is prohibited.

Section 3.3 – Basket Specifications

  • Maximum basket diameter: 18 inches.
  • Maximum basket height: 12 inches.
  • Decorative elements are permitted provided they do not affect scoring.

Article IV: Competition Format

Section 4.1 – Match Duration

Standard competition rounds shall consist of:

  • Fifteen (15) minutes of preparation.
  • Forty-five (45) minutes of underwater weaving.
  • Ten (10) minutes of judging.

Section 4.2 – Start Procedure

Competition begins upon the Head Official's signal. No weaving may commence before the official start.

Section 4.3 – Completion

At the end of the competition period, competitors must immediately cease all weaving activity.

Article V: Judging Criteria

Each basket shall be scored out of 100 points.

Technical Skill (40 points)

Evaluation of:

  • Structural integrity
  • Uniformity
  • Difficulty of weave patterns
  • Material control

Artistic Merit (25 points)

Evaluation of:

  • Creativity
  • Presentation
  • Overall visual appeal

Underwater Execution (25 points)

Evaluation of:

  • Technique
  • Efficiency
  • Composure
  • Adaptability

Sportsmanship (10 points)

Evaluation of:

  • Respect toward competitors and officials
  • Professional conduct
  • Adherence to competition standards

Article VI: Fouls and Penalties

The following actions constitute rule violations:

Minor Infractions

Penalty: Warning or deduction of five (5) points.

Examples include:

  • Delayed arrival to the competition area.
  • Failure to follow official instructions.
  • Excessive disruption of neighboring competitors.

Major Infractions

Penalty: Deduction of fifteen (15) points.

Examples include:

  • Basket tampering.
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct.
  • Intentional interference with another competitor's materials.

Disqualifications

Immediate disqualification may result from:

  • Use of prohibited equipment.
  • Falsification of eligibility information.
  • Dangerous conduct.
  • Intentional sabotage.

Article VII: Athlete Conduct

All competitors shall:

  • Demonstrate integrity and respect.
  • Accept the decisions of officials.
  • Promote a positive image of the sport.
  • Encourage fellow competitors.

Failure to uphold these standards may result in disciplinary review.

Back to Top

Article VIII: Officials

Each sanctioned competition shall include:

  • One (1) Head Official.
  • Two (2) Technical Judges.
  • One (1) Safety Officer.
  • One (1) Scorekeeper.

Officials possess final authority regarding interpretation of these rules.

Article IX: Championships

The annual NYSUBWA State Championship shall determine the Association Champion.

In the event of a scoring tie:

  1. Technical Skill scores shall be compared.
  2. Underwater Execution scores shall be compared.
  3. A Sudden Weave Challenge shall be conducted.

Article X: Spirit of the Sport

Underwater basket weaving celebrates craftsmanship, perseverance, creativity, and community. While competition is encouraged, participants are reminded that the ultimate goal of the Association is the advancement and enjoyment of the sport throughout New York State.


"Excellence Through Tradition, Beneath the Surface."
