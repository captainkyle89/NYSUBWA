About Us

Learn more about the New York State Underwater Basket Weaving Association and the mission that drives our members.

Our Mission

The mission of the New York State Underwater Basket Weaving Association is to advance the sport of underwater basket weaving by promoting excellence, sportsmanship, and community throughout New York State. We are committed to developing athletes, fostering teamwork, and creating opportunities for individuals of all skill levels to compete, learn, and grow. Through education, competition, and dedication to our craft, we strive to inspire the next generation of underwater weavers and ensure the continued growth of this unique sport.

Our Story

Founded in 2019, out NYSUBWA team was created to bring together the best of the best from across New York who share a passion for underwater basket weaving. Through competitions, training, and community events, our members strive to grow the sport while fostering camaraderie and tradition. Our two Team members, Liz and Dennis, have had a growing impact in the sport since 2019, when our team was founded. Both found a natural talent and skill in the art of underwater weaving, and since then have won countless awards and trophies to show for it. They love the sport and wish to watch it grow, and want to share their talents with the people.
